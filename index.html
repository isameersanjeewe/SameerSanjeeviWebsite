The story

The short version

I've shipped products in IoT, Fortune 20 healthcare, and pharma. The domain changes; the job doesn't: find the real problem, set a measurable bar for done, and get engineering, compliance, and the business shipping against it. Regulated healthcare taught me to put quality gates on AI before launch, not after, and that discipline travels to any product with real users and real stakes.

I stay hands-on with the technology I manage. I built jobfinder-v3, an AI agent that scores 500+ job postings a week with the Claude API, because the tool I wanted didn't exist. It keeps my calls on LLM quality, cost, and evaluation grounded in practice, which makes me a sharper partner to the engineers who build production systems.
